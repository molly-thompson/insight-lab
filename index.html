Methodology

Your assessment is analysed using a weighted scoring model designed to identify patterns in your responses. Each answer contributes differently to several behavioural dimensions. For example, a single response may influence traits such as: Analytical Thinking Collaboration Adaptability Confidence Curiosity Planning As you progress through the assessment, these figures are continually updated to build an overall behavioural profile. The platform uses these dimensions to determine your behavioural patterns and highest-scoring characteristics. Then, your report is generated to provide you personalised feedback and insight into your behavioural traits.

About

InsightLab is a demonstration project created for educational purposes. Assessment results are illustrative only and should not be interpreted as professional psychological or behavioural advice.
