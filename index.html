Methodology

Your assessment is analysed using a weighted scoring model designed to identify patterns in your responses.

Each answer contributes differently to several behavioural dimensions:

  • Analytical Thinking
  • Collaboration
  • Adaptability
  • Confidence
  • Curiosity
  • Planning

As you progress through the assessment, these dimensions are continually updated to build your behavioural profile. Your final report provides personalised feedback based on your highest-scoring characteristics.

About

InsightLab is a demonstration project created for educational purposes. Assessment results are illustrative only and should not be interpreted as professional psychological or behavioural advice.
